A catalogue of the many ways production breaks. Circuit breaker,
bulkhead, and timeout patterns spread from here.

What stayed with me was not the patterns but the **stories**. I still
cite the airline reservation system taken down by a single connection
leak when writing about on-call.

The second edition adds cloud material, but the first edition is
sharper.
