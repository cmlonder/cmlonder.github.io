The novel format felt unnecessary at first, then I understood: if it had
explained the **theory of constraints** with a diagram I would have
forgotten it, and because it told a story I did not.

The one-sentence summary is that every improvement outside the
bottleneck is waste. I thought I knew this, and in practice I kept
optimising things that were not the bottleneck.

The factory examples do not map onto software directly; you have to do
the translation yourself.
