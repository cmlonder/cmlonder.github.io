It argues that staying small can be a strategy, and I needed that
argument made.

But the book repeats its own thesis too much; there is fifty pages of
idea in two hundred pages of text. The cases I write up in Radar prove
the thesis better than the book does.

Three stars. The idea is right, the book is long.
