Fixed time, variable scope. Simple, and harder to apply than it sounds,
because it requires actually accepting a cut in scope.

The most useful piece is the notion of **appetite**: asking how much
time a problem is worth before asking how long it will take. The
estimation argument is usually the wrong question.

Written at Basecamp's scale; in a large organisation you do not apply it
directly, you take pieces.
