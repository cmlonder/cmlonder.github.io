The first place I properly saw feedback loops and delays. Especially the
chapter on **delayed feedback**: as the gap between measurement and
effect grows, the system starts to oscillate.

I remembered that chapter while fighting autoscaling settings, and went
looking for the problem in the delay rather than the threshold. That
turned out to be the right place.

Thin and dense. I read it twice and got more the second time.
