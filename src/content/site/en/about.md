I am a solution architect and software engineer. I spend most of my time on
systems that have to keep working once they get big, and on the practical
question of how to build them alongside agents.

## Why this site exists

Writing is how I find out whether I actually understand something. Half of what
is here is unfinished on purpose — the unfinished parts are the honest ones.

## How it is organised

Four formats, sorted by how finished the thinking is rather than by subject:

- **Essays** are arguments I am ready to defend.
- **Notes** are thinking out loud, often wrong.
- **Playbooks** are decisions I have made more than once.
- **Signals** are links worth exactly two sentences.

Topics cut across all four. A subject can show up as a signal today and an essay
a year later, which is roughly how understanding works.
