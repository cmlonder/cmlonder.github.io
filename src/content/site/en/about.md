I am a solution architect and software engineer. I spend most of my time on
systems that have to keep working once they get big, and on the practical
question of how to build them alongside agents.

## Why this site exists

Writing is how I find out whether I actually understand something. This site is
kept in the open so the half-finished parts stay visible — that is the point,
not an accident.

## How it is organised

Four formats, sorted by how finished the thinking is rather than by subject:
essays are arguments I am ready to defend, notes are thinking out loud,
playbooks are decisions I have made more than once, and signals are links worth
two sentences.
