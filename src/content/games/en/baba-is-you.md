The rules sit on the board as objects and you can push them around to rewrite
them. Break up the `BABA IS YOU` blocks into `WALL IS YOU` and you become the
wall.

It is the best account of **metaprogramming** I have come across in years. The
idea that a rule is itself data lands far more clearly here than in any
explanation of a language.

Some levels are unfairly hard; there is one that had me stuck for two weeks and
the solution still feels like a cheat.
