Made by one person: code, art, sound, design. And what came out is the most
original **deduction mechanic** in games.

The game tells you nothing until you get three people right at once. That is a
design decision refusing to reward partial correctness, and it is a brave one:
it makes guessing useless and forces you to actually think.

The 1-bit graphics are not a budget constraint but a design decision. One of
the cleanest examples of a constraint turning into an aesthetic.
