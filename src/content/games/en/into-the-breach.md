Most turn-based games generate tension with dice. This one does the opposite:
it **shows you the enemy's next move outright** and every turn is still a
disaster.

Remove the uncertainty and what is left is pure tradeoff: which of these two
buildings are you sacrificing. It is a familiar feeling from post-mortems.

It was made by a team of eight who followed FTL with a completely different
game, which is its own kind of achievement.
