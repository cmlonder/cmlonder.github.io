The mechanic is simple: check documents, find the inconsistency, stamp. But a
new rule arrives every day and the checklist stops fitting in your head.

What disturbed me was that at some point I started checking **fields** rather
than people. That is precisely what it is about.

It is the example I use when explaining how layers of rules accumulate in
software until nobody knows the whole system.
