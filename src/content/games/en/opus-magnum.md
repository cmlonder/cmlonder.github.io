Every time you finish a level it shows three histograms — cost, cycles, area —
and where your solution sits against your friends'.

The real lesson is that **you cannot optimise all three at once.** The cheap
solution is slow, the fast one takes up space. I do not know anywhere else
that makes architectural tradeoff this concrete.

All of Zachtronics' games work the same idea, but this is the most elegant one.
