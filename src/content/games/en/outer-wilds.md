Your character does not get stronger and you collect no equipment. The only way
to progress is **to understand something**. You could finish the game in the
first minute; you just do not know how.

It might be the only game where learning is translated directly into mechanics.
And because you cannot unlearn what you know, it cannot be replayed — a cost
the design accepts openly.

The twenty-two minute loop is a lesson in how a constraint can carry a
narrative.
