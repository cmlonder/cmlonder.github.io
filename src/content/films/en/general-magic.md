In 1994 they described the smartphone: touchscreen, app store, email, emoji.
All of it worked. The company died.

What makes the documentary valuable is that it shows **timing** as a condition
rather than a skill. The team contained people who later built the iPhone,
eBay and Android. Same people, same idea, different decade.

It is the most expensive example of the "good technology, bad distribution"
pattern I keep writing about in Radar. There was no network, no battery, and
nobody wanted to carry it.
