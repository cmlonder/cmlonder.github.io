It gets remembered as a technology film but there is almost no technology in
it. What it is about is **the ownership structure**: who took what share, how
the dilution happened, who was not invited to which meeting.

For anyone thinking about starting something alone, that is the real lesson. I
see far more ventures fall apart over ambiguity in the partnership than over
the product failing.

Three stars because the film is too in love with its own pace; every scene runs
at the same tempo and after a while you stop caring who is right.
