It looks like a documentary about a sushi master but it is really a film about
**repetition**. Jiro makes the same ten pieces every day and after sixty years
still finds something to correct.

The scene that stayed with me is the rice: an apprentice washes rice and
nothing else for five years. Software has no learning path with that much
patience in it; we say "senior" after three months. The first time I watched
it I found that romantic. Now I read it more as a **definition of expertise**:
you are a master when you know how many different ways the work can go wrong.

Its weakness is that the film glides too gently over what this life costs. What
the sons describe is not as lovely as the framing suggests.
