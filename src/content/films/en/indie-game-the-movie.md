Three developers, three games, three different points of collapse. The panic on
the *Super Meat Boy* team's faces while watching the server on launch day is
the most honest picture of solo work I have seen.

What is interesting is that none of them talks about marketing; all of them
talk about the product. The film was shot in 2012, when distribution was still
the store's job. If the same games shipped at the same quality today they would
probably go unseen — I watch that happen every week in Radar.

Phil Fish's section has aged into something painful. The film has an ending it
does not know about.
