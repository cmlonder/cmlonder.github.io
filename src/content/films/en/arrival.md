A film about **representation** dressed up as a film about aliens. Learning the
language changes thought; learning the model changes the problem itself.

That feels very familiar. The moment you start measuring a system its
behaviour changes, because the team starts working to the metric. I spent the
film thinking about Goodhart's law, which was probably not the intention.

Villeneuve's least showy film and, I think, his best.
